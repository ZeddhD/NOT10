How to Play NOT10

Be the last player with money. Don't be the one who pushes the table to 10.

1

Bet. Everyone bets in secret, $100+ at a time. Biggest bettor wins the biggest pot share and picks who goes first.

2

Play. Take turns dropping one card. Its number adds to the shared table total.

3

Survive. Push the total to 10+ on your turn and you're out. Everyone left splits the pot.
