How to Play NOT10

Objective

Be the last player with money. Avoid being the one to push the table total to 10 or higher.

Setup

  • 2-4 players start with $1,000 each
  • 40-card deck: 10 cards each of values 0, 1, 2, 3
  • Cards dealt: 4 cards (3-4 players) or 6 cards (2 players)
  • Starting player chosen randomly, then rotates clockwise

Betting Rules

  • Minimum bet: $100 (or all-in if you have less)
  • If you have less than $100, you MUST go all-in
  • Must make at least ONE bet action before you can finalize
  • Bet increments: +$100, +$200, +$500 only
  • You can bet multiple times before finalizing
  • CALL matches highest bet and auto-finalizes
  • ALL-IN bets everything and auto-finalizes
  • FINALIZE locks your bet (only available after betting once)
  • Round starts when ALL players finalize

Playing Cards

  • Players take turns clockwise playing one card
  • Table total increases by the card's value
  • Card colors: 0=White, 1=Green, 2=Blue, 3=Red
  • If total reaches 10 or higher after YOUR card → You're ELIMINATED
  • Only one player eliminated per round

Pot Distribution & Play Order

  • WEIGHTED POT: Share proportional to your bet amount!
  • Formula: Your Share = (Your Bet ÷ Survivor Bets) × Pot
  • Example: Bet $500, others bet $100 each → You get 50%+ of pot!
  • Big bets = BIG rewards if you survive (but big losses if you bust)
  • Small bets = Small rewards (safe but less profit)
  • HIGHEST BETTOR CHOOSES POSITION = Go FIRST or LAST!
  • GO FIRST: Apply pressure, show strength (best with low cards)
  • GO LAST: See all cards first, perfect info (best with risky cards)
  • Eliminated player gets $0
  • If your money reaches $0 → Spectator
  • Game ends when one player has all the money

Strategic Implications

  • Strong hand (0s, 1s): BET BIG! Get huge pot share + choose FIRST to intimidate
  • Weak hand (2s, 3s): Bet big to choose LAST position = see all cards
  • Position choice: Adds skill-based decision making
  • Mind games: Why did they choose first? Bluff or strength?
  • Risk/Reward: Calculate if big bet is worth potential loss
  • Fewer survivors: Larger shares for everyone

Bot Personalities

  • Carl (Cautious): Min bets, finalizes early (60-80%)
  • Betty (Balanced): Moderate strategy (40-60% finalize)
  • Alex (Aggressive): High bets, finalizes late (30-50%)
